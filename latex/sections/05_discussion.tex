\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

We set out to measure whether LLMs answer air pollution policy questions as
reliably for the Global South as for the Global North, whether prompt framing
changes the answer, and what mechanism drives any gap. Across 25 countries, 14
models, four languages, five tasks and two personas, scored against official
ground truth by code where the answer is a register value and by a three-vendor
panel elsewhere, the harms we can demonstrate are practical: the three remedies
an agency would reach for all fail, the most intuitive of them makes the answer
materially worse, and models are least reliable on the recall of the binding
value in force. A North/South tier difference persists, and the mechanism
behind it is not established at the level where inference is sound.

\subsection{Three intuitive remedies, all failing, and one that harms}

The local language does not help and materially hurts. Asking in the country's
own language lowers the composite by $\nhtwomixedcountry$~pp with a random
intercept per country, significantly in all three languages and with the country
as the unit of inference, and the largest
penalty falls on Hindi, the language with the scarcest corpus, with India, the
highest-scoring country of all 25 in English, falling furthest. This is the
direction the resource hierarchy predicts~\citep{joshi2020state,xue2021mt5},
and the measurement can see it because the unit is the language rather than
the country; with three languages, and Spanish and Portuguese indistinguishable,
the ordering is descriptive. The effect depends on no single country, model,
task, persona condition or scoring instrument, nor on translation quality, and
it holds on an outcome involving no judge at all: the model becomes markedly
less likely to produce a checkable number in the first place, and more likely
to return nothing.

The local-manager persona is equivalent to no persona. The
difference-in-differences is $\ndid$~pp (permutation $p=\npersonap$) and its
country-level interval sits inside a $\pm2$~pp margin, so this is an
equivalence result rather than an underpowered null, consistent with evidence
that expert personas do not improve factual
accuracy~\citep{zheng2024helpful,mollick2025personas}, and its main effect is a
small loss ($\npersonamain$~pp). This null carries more weight than a null
about a folk habit would, because role framing is the first thing every major
vendor tells its users to do; Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI converge on the
same advice (Section~\ref{sec:related}), so that an administration following
its supplier's documentation writes the sentence we tested. What that
documentation claims for the sentence is control over tone, format and framing,
and nothing in it says that the technique which shapes register does not also
shape factual reliability. Our estimate separates the two. A manipulation check
confirms that half the responses explicitly take up the role, so the frame is
received and changes the voice; it does not change what the model knows. We
tested it typed into the message rather than installed as a system
instruction, the channel a public servant in a chat interface has
(Section~\ref{sec:limits}).

The regional model buys nothing over the model it was tuned from. On the
register values, re-served on the same Portuguese items, Cabra returns the value
in force \ncabsanitycabra\% of the time and its base model
\ncabsanitybase\%: instruction tuning on Brazilian Portuguese did not improve
what the model recalls about Brazilian regulation. It also ranks last of the
fourteen and trails its scale-matched peer on the Portuguese prompts it was
tuned for ($\nhthreebra$ against $\nhthreebrallama$ for Llama~3.1~8B;
$\nhthreenarrow$~pp across the 30 Portuguese prompts), but those comparisons run
on the composite, whose judged subcomponents come from the collection judges,
and the roster rank compares a 7B model with systems an order of magnitude
larger. The defensible claim is the narrow one, and it is the one an agency
needs: downloading a regionally tuned small model does not recover the national
facts a larger model misses. Two limits bound it. The narrow test is 30 items
per model, and the model we could run is a community fine-tune of a 7B base, not
a commercial regional frontier model of the kind an agency might procure. For those the finding is a prediction, and the released protocol
is the way to test it.

Practitioners deploy all three as fixes. One does active damage, one is
equivalent to doing nothing, and one adds nothing to the model it started from. For Global South environmental
agencies, no cheap prompt- or model-level workaround substitutes for verifying
the binding value against the official source.

\subsection{The recall floor sits on the values that bind}

The risk falls unevenly. A manager drafting prose about air quality policy is
comparatively well served; a manager retrieving the regulatory cutoff a
decision turns on is in the worst cell of the design
(Section~\ref{sec:results:task}), and worse still if they ask in a
lower-resource language. The floor is not an artefact of scoring: the factual
verdict on both recall tasks is decided by code against the register, the
contrast holds with every model and every country as the unit, and a third of
the wrong answers on the national standard cite a value from the country's own
official ladder rather than an invented one. The models know the ladder and not
which rung binds.

\subsection{A tier difference we can show, and a gradient we cannot}

The geographic finding is a difference between groups, not a slope. On the
outcomes no judge touches, Global South countries return the register value
$\ncodegap$~pp less often than Global North countries, with the country as the
unit; on the composite the gap is $\ntiergap$~pp, the same size inside the
extension wave and on the pre-specified fifteen, and unchanged when the four EU
members count as one. It is concentrated: it scales with how much the answer
depends on a fact specific to the country and is absent on the one task with
no register value to get right (Section~\ref{sec:results:h1}), although the
per-task intervals with the country as the unit are wide and that
decomposition is exploratory. The development gradient stays below the
pre-specified threshold on every reading, and its interval, $[\nrhocilo,\nrhocihi]$,
includes both zero and the threshold: 25 countries neither establish a gradient
nor rule one out. The country ordering shows why the two results differ. India
tops all 25 countries despite being Global South, Canada and France sit low, so
the disadvantage is a tier difference layered over country-specific
idiosyncrasy that a rank correlation over 25 countries cannot see through.

\subsection{The mechanism, and why it is not established}

The pre-specified mechanism test fails, and part of the reason is a defect in
the instrument. The proxy assigns the size of the Wikipedia edition in a
country's dominant official language, so that nine of our 25 countries receive
the value of the English Wikipedia. The variable does not measure how much text
exists in a country's language; it measures whether the country has English as
an official language, which tracks colonial history rather than corpus size. We
report this because it bears on any study that has used or will use the same
proxy.

Changing the unit of variation buys one result and refuses another. At the
country level the deficit on country-specific tasks is predicted by development
($\beta=\nhfourhdialonebeta$, $p=\nhfourhdialoneclp$ clustered;
$\rho=\nhfourctryrhohdi$, $p=\nhfourctryphdi$ across the 25 deficits) and not,
at conventional levels, by encyclopedic coverage ($p=\nhfourclp$;
$\rho=\nhfourctryrho$, $p=\nhfourctryp$). The two correlate at
$\rho=\nrhocobhdi$ and the design has no lever that moves one without the
other, so this does not identify development as the operative channel; it says
that the exposure which clears the threshold is the one the pre-specified
mechanism was meant to rule out.

The result is also narrower than the gradient it superficially resembles.
Development does not predict how accurately models answer about a country
(Section~\ref{sec:results:h1}); it predicts how much worse they answer the
questions whose answer is a fact about that country. The two are different
outcomes, and only the second has a country-level association. What remains
excluded is the language-corpus channel, as it must be for a contrast measured
entirely in English.

\subsection{The methodological lesson: the key matters more than the sample}

The same scored cells support different substantive conclusions depending on
the instrument used to score them, and the size of that difference is the
lesson. Scoring two tasks against placeholder keys and delegating range
arithmetic to a judge yielded a significant development gradient and a small
language penalty; rebuilding the keys from official registers and moving the
comparison into code halved the gradient and more than doubled the penalty
(Sections~\ref{sec:results:h1} and \ref{sec:results:h2}). Moving the factual
verdict on the third register-valued task from two collection judges to code
changed its headline odds ratio by a third, because the judges had been split
along the same line as the tiers. The key itself kept moving under scrutiny:
the United Kingdom entry carried a 2040 target in place of the limit in force,
and the Bangladesh entry carried the 2005 value after the 2022 Rules had raised
it, so that a model citing the correct current standard for a Global South
country was being scored wrong until an external reader caught it. The dataset
never changed; only the instrument did. The field's instinct when a
geographic-bias effect proves fragile is to add countries, but the ground truth
deserves the scrutiny first. A defective key does not merely add noise; it adds
structured noise, because the defects concentrate wherever official texts are
hardest to obtain or most recently revised, which is where the hypothesis lives.
The released registry therefore carries a content hash (Supplementary Material)
so that any reader can tell which version of the key a number was computed
against. The same lesson applies to inference: with the response as the unit,
H4 had $p=\nhfourp$; with the country as the unit it has $p=\nhfourctryp$, and
only the second describes what 25 countries can show.

\subsection{Why the domain matters}

Fine particulate matter is among the leading environmental risk factors for
premature mortality, and the burden falls disproportionately on the Global
South: the World Health Organization attributes \num{88548} deaths in Brazil in
2021 to ambient air pollution (uncertainty interval
\numrange{69477}{108431})~\citep{who2026gho_air41}, and short-term exposure
continues to drive measurable mortality in Brazilian
cities~\citep{requia2024shortterm}. When a manager delegates a normative lookup
or an episode recommendation to an LLM, an inaccurate answer can propagate into
a regulatory brief or an emergency response.

We do not claim the model is least reliable where consequences are greatest,
because we did not measure it: disease burden is not among our country
covariates, and the one country-level observation we have points the other way,
since India carries one of the largest PM\textsubscript{2.5} mortality burdens
in the world and is also the highest-scoring country in our sample. Whether
reliability is misaligned with need is answerable by adding an age-standardised
attributable-mortality covariate, but not by this design. The
coloniality-of-knowledge framework~\citep{mohamed2020decolonial} offers one
interpretation of the pattern we did measure, in which LLMs are compressed
summaries of corpora produced under uneven digital and publication access and
the language penalty is a link from corpus asymmetry to knowledge asymmetry; the
data support the penalty and the tier difference, and the interpretation
remains an interpretation.

\subsection{Limitations and future opportunities}
\label{sec:limits}

Each limit below bounds this design, and each names the study that would remove
it. We list them in the order we would run them.

\emph{No retrieval arm.} Every answer comes from the model's parametric memory.
A manager using a search-augmented assistant, or a retrieval system over the
national gazette, faces a different instrument, and the gap and the language
penalty may both change under it. The released prompts and registries allow a
retrieval arm to be run on the same items, and it is the first study we would
add.

\emph{Role framing was tested in the user turn, not the system prompt.} The
manager frame is prefixed to the message rather than installed as a system
instruction, where three of the four vendor guides place it; Google's admits
either that slot or the start of the user prompt, which is the one we used. The
choice follows the population we care about, public servants in the chat
interface, who have no system prompt to configure, but our null bounds that
user's channel, not a developer's. Whether a system-instruction persona recovers
factual accuracy where a typed one does not is a two-cell experiment on the same
items.

\emph{From panel-scored to gold-anchored.} No human expert re-scored responses
here. Three properties bound what that costs: where the answer is a register
value the factual verdict is computed by code; where judgement is required the
score is the mean of three vendors rather than one; and every reported effect is
a between-group contrast, in which a judge's systematic severity cancels.
Restricting to objective factual accuracy strengthens both headline effects, the
opposite of what a permissive judge would produce. Two residues remain: the four
non-factual subcomponents of a T1 cell are still the collection judge's, which
is why the code verdict and not the composite is the primary tier outcome, and
the extractor's manual audit covered T1 only. A blinded human-expert validation
of a stratified sample would convert an official-source-anchored result into a
gold-standard-anchored one. The sample is exported with the release
(\texttt{code/analysis/export\_human\_validation.py}): 150 responses
stratified by tier, language and task, three rating sheets carrying the prompt,
the answer and the official key but not the model, the tier or the machine's
score, and a key file opened only after the sheets return.

\emph{From a within-country signal to a causal design.} Within countries we
remove country-level confounding without removing task-by-country confounding,
and the signal does not reach significance at the country level. A design that
varies country coverage while holding development fixed, or a longitudinal
study tracking accuracy as a country's encyclopedic footprint grows, would
address the first problem; more countries would address the second.

\emph{From a null gradient to an adequately powered test.} The tier difference
is established while the gradient is not, at $n=25$, and the gradient's
interval includes the threshold. A pre-registered replication at larger $n$,
which the open dataset makes cheap, would settle whether the gradient is absent
or underpowered.

\emph{From three native languages to the full multilingual matrix.} The
strongest result rests on three native languages, so its mechanism is reported
descriptively even though the effect is not; for one of the three, Hindi, the
official instrument is itself published in English, so the penalty there is a
penalty for the user's language rather than for the language of the register.
The translation-quality objection is settled empirically by the back-translation
census reported above; what remains open is breadth, and scaling to more
languages is the highest-value extension this study points to.

\emph{From one domain to a cross-domain program.} All effects come from one
high-stakes applied domain and one corpus family (Wikimedia), and we confine
the mechanistic language accordingly. The same task suite and
ground-truth-registry method transfer directly to other regulated,
source-anchored domains such as water quality, food safety and vaccine policy.

\emph{Served-model stack as the unit of analysis.} We evaluate models as served
through fixed access stacks, which is what a public manager queries; a residual
gap could in principle reflect the stack rather than the weights. The serving
check on the regional model (Section~\ref{sec:results:robust}) covers one stack;
a controlled design with identical open weights across stacks would separate
the two for all of them, which the per-call provenance we release enables.

\subsection{What this study contributes}

The three fixes practitioners reach for first fail together, each in its own
way: the local language harms, the persona frame is equivalent to no frame, and
regional tuning at 7B adds nothing to the base model it started from. The
methodological result is that in this literature the ground-truth key, and the
level at which inference is carried, matter more than the sample size; changing
those two, without touching a single response, moved a significant development
gradient to a null, doubled the language penalty, and turned an established
corpus mechanism into an open question. And the design separates what 25
countries can demonstrate from what they cannot, reporting the second set as
open rather than as supported.
